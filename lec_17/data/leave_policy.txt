Leave Policy

All full-time employees receive 20 paid annual leave days per calendar year.
Annual leave accrues monthly and unused days may be carried over up to a
maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year, after which they expire.

Sick leave is granted separately, with 10 paid sick days per year. A medical
certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.

Employees must submit leave requests through the HR portal at least 5
working days in advance for planned leave. Emergency leave may be approved
retroactively at the manager's discretion.
