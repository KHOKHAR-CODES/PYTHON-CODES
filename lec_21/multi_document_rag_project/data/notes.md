# Employee Handbook Notes

## Resignation Notice
Employees must provide **30 days** written notice before resigning from
their position. The notice period may be shortened by mutual agreement
between the employee and their manager.

## Remote Work
Remote work is allowed up to **two days per week**, subject to manager
approval. Fully remote arrangements require HR sign-off and are evaluated
on a case-by-case basis.

## Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday,
with a one-hour lunch break.
